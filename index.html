    Exam format

    SectionWhatTimeWeight
    I40 multiple-choice questions80 min50%
    II4 free-response: Mathematical Routines · Translation Between Representations · Experimental Design and Analysis · Qualitative/Quantitative Translation100 min50%
    • You get an equation sheet and can use a calculator on the whole exam. Learn where things are on the sheet so you don't waste time hunting.
    • Write the equation with symbols first, then plug in numbers. Graders give points for the setup even if the arithmetic slips.
    • "Justify" or "explain" means cite a law or equation and connect it to the situation in a full sentence.
    • On Experimental Design, name what you measure, what you change, what you keep constant, and how you'd graph it to get a straight line.
    • Do scaling questions with ratios ("r doubles, so F goes to ¼"), not by plugging in made-up numbers.
    • Check units and size of every answer. A 108 m/s ball means something went wrong.
    • Skim all four FRQs first and start with the one you're most sure of.

    Format is for the redesigned course (first tested May 2025). Confirm the 2027 date and details on AP Central.
